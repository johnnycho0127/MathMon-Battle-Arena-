📖 How to Play MathMon Card Battle

  1. Scan Your Physical Cards: You and your friend each bring a card, scan it with your phone camera (or upload a photo)—the scanner automatically reads the Character Name & Real HP on the card and brings the character alive on the fighting stage!
  2. Ask a Math Question: One player asks a math problem (for example, 3 + 3).
  3. Beat the 1-Minute Timer: The other player has 1:00 (60 seconds) to solve it! Answer 6 in time and your character attacks for 6 damage (60 HP → 54 HP).
  4. Wrong Answer / Time's Up Rule: If a player gets the wrong answer (or runs out of the 1:00 timer), the game points out the exact correct answer amount and deducts that amount from their HP via an automatic counter-attack!
